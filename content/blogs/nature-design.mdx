Welcome to our first blog post! This is where we discuss our transition to a Natural Design System.

## The Core Philosophy

Nature doesn't have sharp edges, and neither should our UI. We rely heavily on soft curves, dynamic colors, and smooth transitions to provide a relaxing user experience.

### Typography

Our typography uses Space Grotesk and Inter to ensure everything remains readable while still conveying a unique personality.
