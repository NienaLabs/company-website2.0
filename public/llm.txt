## 1. Executive Summary & Company Thesis

### The Core Problem
The tech industry has spent years confining artificial intelligence inside web browsers, consumer chatbot wrappers, and centralized cloud servers. But the real economy—factories producing goods, distribution centers moving freight, hospitals admitting patients, retail stores stocking merchandise, and commercial fleets operating on roads—runs on physical constraints:
- Millisecond latencies where delayed decisions cause machinery failure or lost inventory.
- Variable lighting, mechanical vibrations, sensor occlusions, and optical noise.
- Strict data sovereignty requirements where confidential records cannot be sent to third-party public cloud APIs.
- Intermittent or nonexistent internet connectivity requiring 100% offline edge resilience.

### The Niena Labs Thesis: "Physical-World Intelligence"
*Software that perceives the physical world. Infrastructure that endures every operational constraint. Hardware that acts with decisive intent.*

Our job is to bring intelligence into the real economy. We engineer full-stack systems bridging mathematical models with physical reality—from custom fine-tuned Large Language Models (LLMs) and expressive conversational avatars to high-speed conveyor vision pipelines and ruggedized edge appliances.

## 4. Flagship Case Study: Compound-V (Retail Perception Engine)

- **Case Study URL:** https://www.nienalabs.com/case-studies/compound-v
- **Status:** v6 Production Deployment
- **Deployment Metrics:**
  - `< 50ms` Local Edge Response Time
  - `85%+` Reduction in False Alarms compared to conventional retail AI
  - `100%` Privacy Compliant (Zero facial recognition, zero biometric databases)
  - `30 FPS` Real-Time Operator Composite HUD Stream

### Technical Architecture
- **Stage 1 (People Perception & 2D Image Zones):** Single-pass multi-person joint regression operating in constant time. Features Top-5 Keypoint Confidence Scoring to prevent shoppers behind carts from being dropped, paired with velocity-normalized association that hands over state across occlusions. Drastically reduces camera calibration overhead by using 2D image-space polygon zones per camera.
- **Stage 2 (Scene & Object Evidence):** Evaluates fixture changes using normalized chromaticity difference in LAB color space ($a^*, b^*$) combined with structural Sobel gradient differences. Eliminates false removal alarms from cast shadows and auto-exposure. Clear-slot gate with timeout and masked fallback prevents deadlocks when customers linger in front of shelves.
- **Stage 3 (Streaming Causal Sequence Model):** Features a 2-layer causal recurrent GRU with persistent hidden state carrying visit history. Multi-head classifier predicts action, risk tier, and item destination with entropy-based abstention.
- **Runtime Arbitration:** State transfers are driven strictly by confirmed physical object evidence, never by neural action predictions alone. Separate handling for staff-to-customer handovers and POS checkout verification.

## 8. Privacy, Security & Data Sovereignty

- **URL:** https://www.nienalabs.com/terms
- **Data Sovereignty:** Fine-tuned enterprise models run on customer hardware or dedicated private instances without vendor data lock-in. No training on client data.
